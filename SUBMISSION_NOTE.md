# ReciprocityLock v1.1 — Submission Note

ReciprocityLock verifies whether one declared right is truly mirrored across two roles. Party B must accept the pact on-chain before Party A can submit semantic terms. Invalid model output rolls back without consuming an attempt; normalized replay and reserved prompt tokens are rejected; at most five converged attempts are stored. A mirrored verdict activates a deterministic one-shot right that either party may exercise.
